Simple Omlette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This Classic omellete combines beaten eggs coocked to perfection, optionally filled with choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Prepration Time

  • Total:Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2-3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, diced vegetables, coocked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper until well mixed.
  2. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat and add butter or oil.
  3. Pour in the eggs and tilt the pan to spread evenly.
  4. Add fillings when adges set but center is slightly runny.
  5. Fold the omlette and cook for another minute.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy!
